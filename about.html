About Us

Independent publishers of evidence-based practical guides

Middleton & Price was built around a single belief: that well-written, genuinely researched books change how people think and act. Every title in our library goes through the same test — does it contain actionable insight that makes a measurable difference to the reader?

Gareth Price

Publisher & Technology Author

Gareth Price is an entrepreneur and systems thinker with a background in technology and data-driven decision making. He founded Middleton & Price to bring together practical non-fiction across several categories — all united by the same principle: research first, opinion second.

Gareth writes primarily on investing, geopolitics, AI for business, and the psychology of performance. His books are used by business owners, investors, and professionals who want frameworks rather than motivation.

When not writing, he builds the data infrastructure that keeps Middleton & Price's research current — monitoring markets, geopolitical shifts, and emerging trends to ensure every title remains relevant.

Sophia Melrose

Relationships & Psychology Author

Sophia Melrose writes about the psychology of human connection — attachment theory, body language, manipulation, confidence, and the often baffling dynamics of romantic relationships. Her work draws on peer-reviewed research but is written to be immediately useful rather than academically distant.

Her flagship title, Why He Pulls Away: The Attachment Science That Explains Every Confusing Thing Men Do, has helped thousands of women understand the avoidant attachment patterns that cause so much confusion and pain in relationships.

Sophia's writing is characterised by its directness — she doesn't traffic in generalities or hollow affirmations. Every chapter answers a specific question with a specific, evidence-backed answer.

Our Promise

Every book we publish is research-grounded, practically focused, and honestly priced. We don't publish filler. If a guide doesn't earn its place in your library, it doesn't get published.
